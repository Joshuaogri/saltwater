# SW-CANON-002 — Primate City and Global International Hub

**Status:** Adopted as binding Saltwater world canon  
**Adoption date:** 2026-11-25  
**Scope:** All 44 blueprint divisions and every world-building, simulation, data and operational system

## Canonical decision

Saltwater is the country's **single primate city** and dominant national and international metropolis. It is the country's principal concentration point for political authority, corporate power, finance, trade, culture, entertainment, specialist services, technology, education, healthcare, transport and international connectivity.

Saltwater is also a **world-leading international transit, exchange, export and logistics hub**. Its maritime and commercial strategic importance is comparable in role (not necessarily physical design) to the Suez Canal and the world's largest port, transshipment, financial and commercial centres.

This is not an ordinary small city with an unusually large number of businesses. It is a globally consequential city-state whose international economic reach, institutional scale, corporate presence, cargo throughput, passenger flows and daily transient population can be many times larger than its permanent resident population.

## Required city character

The highest national and international levels of every major sector must be represented in Saltwater, including:

- **Government and diplomacy:** national government, central agencies, diplomatic missions, consulates, international organisations and major public institutions.
- **Finance:** leading domestic and international banks, central financial institutions, investment banks, capital markets, insurance, reinsurance, asset management, foreign exchange, trade finance and commodity finance.
- **Corporate headquarters:** national headquarters, regional headquarters, global offices, major subsidiaries and strategic command centres of large domestic and international companies.
- **Maritime and shipping:** global shipping lines, port authorities, container terminals, transshipment operators, ship agencies, marine services, shipbuilding and repair, bunkering, maritime insurance and vessel support.
- **Export and international trade:** export industries, processing, manufacturing, wholesale markets, import/export houses, commodity exchanges, customs brokerage, freight forwarding and re-export operations.
- **Logistics and distribution:** major warehouses, distribution centres, bonded facilities, cold chains, inland cargo terminals, supply-chain control centres and multimodal freight operators.
- **Transport and aviation:** international passenger and cargo airport functions, major passenger terminals, rail and road interchange, port/airport connections and international transport operators.
- **Entertainment and culture:** major film, television, music, streaming, publishing, fashion, sports, gaming, live-event, theatre, exhibition and convention industries.
- **Technology and communications:** major technology firms, telecommunications, data infrastructure, research, innovation, digital commerce and international media.
- **Hospitality and tourism:** international hotel groups, luxury and business accommodation, convention tourism, major visitor attractions and high-capacity event facilities.
- **Healthcare and education:** leading specialist hospitals, medical research, universities, international education providers and major training institutions.
- **Professional and specialist services:** global and national legal, accounting, consulting, engineering, design, recruitment, security, scientific and technical firms.

## Population and scale rule

The established **10,000-person permanent city population cap remains unchanged**. It is a limit on distinct people counted under the adopted population canon; it is **not** a limit on:

- corporate size, number of companies, subsidiaries, branches or establishments;
- institutional scale, number of facilities or volume of economic activity;
- daily visitors, tourists, passengers, international crews, traders or other transient people, subject to the population canon's distinct-person counting rules when physically present;
- cargo volume, vessel movements, container throughput, exports, imports, transshipment or financial flows;
- international reach, market value, corporate influence or global importance.

Permanent residents, temporary visitors, commuters, crews and workers must be modelled using the existing population-counting rules. Do not silently create additional person records or bypass the 10,000-person cap.

## Business registry interpretation

The existing 10,000-business registry is an initial generated registry baseline, not a ceiling on Saltwater's corporate scale or a claim that the city has only small local firms. Its sector mix, business profiles, organisation hierarchy, establishments and operating patterns must be developed to represent a global primate city.

Represent large groups accurately through distinct parent organisations, subsidiaries, branches, headquarters, regional offices, operating companies and establishments where the data model supports them. Distinguish a legal business entity from its individual establishments and facilities. Do not inflate the permanent population when adding companies or business locations.

## Built-world and simulation consequences

All future and revisited systems must reflect this canon:

1. **Property and land use:** premium central business districts, global corporate headquarters, major financial towers, large mixed-use complexes, international hotels, entertainment precincts, industrial estates, export-processing areas, bonded logistics zones, port estates and airport cargo districts.
2. **Transport and geography:** international maritime approaches, major port and transshipment capacity, airport and cargo operations, freight rail/road corridors, passenger interchange and high-capacity connections between the port, airport, industrial zones and commercial core.
3. **Businesses and institutions:** global-scale operators and specialist institutions across every major sector, not a retail-heavy or neighbourhood-only economy.
4. **Operating patterns:** continuous and shift-based operations, night-time cargo movements, vessel and flight schedules, round-the-clock financial and logistics functions, major events and substantial visitor flows.
5. **Police simulation:** international visitors and crews, major transport and commercial sites, cargo and port security, financial and corporate crime, large events, diplomatic locations, critical infrastructure, complex jurisdictional coordination and high-volume transient activity.
6. **Economic modelling:** distinguish permanent population from workforce presence, visitors, passengers, cargo, vessel movements, trade value and institutional/corporate scale.

## Binding implementation rule

This canon applies across all 44 blueprint divisions. When a later design, dataset, generator, test, narrative or interface conflicts with this decision, revise that implementation to fit this canon; do not downgrade Saltwater to an ordinary small city. Preserve the one-city-only setting and the 10,000-person cap. No separate approval is required for routine fictional world-building needed to implement this adopted canon.
