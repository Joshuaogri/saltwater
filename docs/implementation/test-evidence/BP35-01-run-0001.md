## BP35-01 acceptance coverage

Verified in this increment:
- Explicit registered handler, owner-domain, event-code and schema-version contract.
- Running/player-present intake and dispatch gate.
- Stable idempotency and conflict rejection.
- Deterministic due-tick/priority/event-ID ordering.
- Causal prerequisite blocking, duplicate dependency idempotency and cycle rejection.
- Claim-token ownership and stale-token rejection.
- Explicit authorizer; denial quarantines before owner-handler invocation.
- Owner-handler success requires a durable outcome reference.
- Handler exceptions and invalid results do not become false successes.
- Bounded retries and poison-event quarantine.
- Pause cannot commit while an event-work claim remains active; pause succeeds after claims finish.
- Append-only attempt evidence and rollback of acceptance fixtures.

The package does not claim completion of all 230 BP-35 design acceptance criteria. NPC orchestration, full causal world simulation, fairness/back-pressure, deterministic random streams, replay, automatic abandoned-claim recovery and full observability remain later work.
