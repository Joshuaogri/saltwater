## Implementation scope

Proposal 0010 adds business-owned identity and append-only business lifecycle history, an immutable cross-domain provisioning manifest and a BUSINESS adapter in the existing SW-UNS naming registry. It is a disposable proposal, not a numbered source migration and not an applied Neon migration.

The deterministic fixture creates 9,000 resident-cohort people and 500 police-subset-cohort people, all as distinct person identities counted by the existing population counter. The 500 headroom remains unused. The police subset is a test-only cohort label: no police personnel appointments, ranks, units or career records are inserted.

The fixture creates 10,000 distinct businesses with globally reserved canonical names. Category/legal-form/staff-band variety exists only in a sw_test sidecar with explicit TEST_ labels and NON_GEOGRAPHIC_TEST_FIXTURE. No BP-04 distribution canon or detailed geography is invented; all business sites remain unassigned. Only the two existing Saltwater geographic roots are present.
